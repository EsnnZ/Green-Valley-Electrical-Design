# Green valley mining site office and workshop electrical distribution design
Self-directed electrical distribution design for a conceptual WA mining office and workshop, covering HV/LV topology, load analysis, cable sizing, power factory studies.

## Project objective

Develop a electrical distribution design and demonstrate application of AS/NZS electrical standards and power system analysis tools.

## System architecture

**33 kV - 11 kV - 415 V**

### Major equipment

- 33/11 kV transformer
- 11 kV distribution feeder
- RMU
- 11/0.415 kV transformer
- 415 V MSB
- Office DB
- Workshop DB

## Current Progress

### Completed

- Preliminary electrical topology / single line model
- Equipment and load schedule development
- Maximum-demand analysis based on AS/NZS 3000
- Maximum demand Rev 01:
  - 54.1 kW
  - 30.9 kVAr
  - 62.3 kVA
  - 0.868 lagging PF

### In progress

- LV cable sizing
- AS/NZS 3008 application
- Voltage drop assessment

### Planned

- short circuit behavior study
